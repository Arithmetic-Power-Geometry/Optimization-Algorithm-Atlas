# Mechanism Similarity Analysis Protocol

The initial diagnostic uses equal-weight fieldwise Jaccard similarity over normalized categorical fingerprints.

Before publication: justify features; separate family-defining from problem-scope fields; test alternative weights and distances; report nearest-neighbor/cluster sensitivity; compare against known genealogy; inspect disagreements manually; distinguish structural similarity from empirical behavioral similarity.

High structural similarity is a novelty-audit signal, not proof of redundancy. Low similarity does not imply superior novelty or performance.
