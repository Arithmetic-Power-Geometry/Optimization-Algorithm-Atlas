# Presentation Blueprint

The final review should be navigable at three depths.

## 30-second view
One visual answers: What families exist, what information do they use, and where is evidence dense or missing?

## 5-minute researcher view
A mechanism genealogy, evidence-status heatmap and benchmark-property map let a researcher locate the nearest algorithm, relevant comparator and unresolved conditions.

## Deep-audit view
Machine-readable cards expose sources, conditions, implementations, contradictory evidence and resolving experiments.

## Planned signature visuals
1. Optimization Mechanism River — historical flow of mechanism ideas rather than metaphor names.
2. Algorithm DNA Map — algorithms positioned by information use, state, memory, geometry and adaptation.
3. Evidence Constellation — algorithm × condition status heatmap.
4. Benchmark Blind-Spot Map — which landscape properties are over/under represented.
5. Failure-Frontier Atlas — experimentally observed capability boundaries.
6. Structural Redundancy Network — close mechanisms despite different names.
7. Reproducibility Ladder — definition → code → rerun → independent reproduction.
8. Research Frontier Map — each unresolved cell linked to a concrete resolving experiment.

## Main-paper discipline
Avoid hundreds of disconnected algorithm paragraphs. Synthesize by mechanism and evidence status; use algorithm cards as supplementary atlas material.
