# Cross-Dimensional Checkpoint Scaling

This artifact is a descriptive precursor to the official target-runtime/ECDF component of R10.

It combines the validated D5, D10, D20, and D40 function-level checkpoint summaries on the common normalized budgets 100, 300, and 1000 FE/D.

Raw objective magnitudes remain function-specific. The artifact must not be used to average objective values across BBOB functions or to claim an overall optimizer winner.

The table preserves every algorithm × function × dimension × normalized-budget cell, including explicit UNOBSERVED cells. Its role is to expose dimension-conditioned patterns and candidate regions for later failure-frontier discovery.

Official COCO observer/logger data remain authoritative for target attainment and target-runtime ECDF analysis.
