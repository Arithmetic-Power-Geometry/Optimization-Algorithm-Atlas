# Publication Experiment Promotion Gate

No CALIBRATION_ONLY or PILOT_ONLY result may be cited as project evidence.

A new experiment receives PROJECT-VALIDATED status only if all applicable conditions pass:

1. research question and hypothesis are predeclared;
2. benchmark suite/version/instances are frozen;
3. algorithm implementations/versions are pinned;
4. parameter and tuning policies are frozen;
5. boundary and constraint handling are recorded;
6. seed-generation rule and run count are frozen;
7. objective/time budgets are frozen;
8. raw outcomes and anytime traces are retained;
9. failure/timeout handling is predeclared;
10. statistical analysis is predeclared;
11. deterministic components replay exactly where applicable;
12. stochastic results pass integrity/schema checks;
13. provenance hashes are generated;
14. workflow regenerates all reported artifacts from a clean checkout;
15. results are interpreted only within evaluated conditions.

Promotion is a status transition in the evidence ledger, not a manual change in manuscript prose.
