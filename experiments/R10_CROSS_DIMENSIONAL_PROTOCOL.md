# R10 Cross-Dimensional Analysis Protocol

R10 begins only after R09 is green.

## Fixed dimensions
D = 5, 10, 20, 40. D=2 remains an earlier descriptive checkpoint and is not silently promoted to exact target-runtime evidence.

## Fixed algorithms
RandomSearch, SciPy-DE, SciPy-NelderMead, pycma-CMAES under the frozen comparator configurations.

## Primary cross-dimensional unit
Function × instance × algorithm × dimension, with stochastic seed structure retained. Raw objective magnitudes are never averaged across heterogeneous BBOB functions.

## Target/runtime authority
Official COCO observer/logger data are authoritative for BBOB target attainment and COCO-compatible ECDF/runtime analysis. ObjectiveRecorder remains authoritative for objective-call accounting, checkpoints, runtime diagnostics, failures, and budget invariants.

## Scaling views
- evaluations divided by dimension (FE/D);
- target attainment by declared COCO-compatible target grid;
- ECDF/data-profile style summaries;
- condition-level failure/termination frequency;
- dimension-conditioned descriptive checkpoint summaries.

## Statistical discipline
Matched conditions are preserved. Ranks are descriptive only. Missing/unobserved outcomes remain explicit. No algorithm is removed because of poor performance. No target or dimension is selected after observing comparative results.

## Failure-frontier handoff
R10 may identify candidate dimension/function/target regions for R11 discovery. R11 confirmation must use separately frozen held-out conditions or an independently declared confirmation design; discovery observations are not themselves confirmatory evidence.
