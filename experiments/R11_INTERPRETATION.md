# R11 Interpretation and Integrity Decision

Status: PROJECT-VALIDATED CHARACTERIZATION; BINARY FRONTIER CLAIM NOT PROMOTED

The EF003 discovery campaign and EF005 held-out campaign were executed on disjoint BBOB instance sets with frozen algorithms, dimensions, seeds and FE/D budgets. The computational and provenance pipelines passed, including COCO-authoritative target logging and joint cocopp postprocessing.

The predeclared R11 protocol required an exact target subset and reliability threshold to be fixed before held-out outcomes were inspected. Those exact numerical choices were not frozen in the repository before EF005 execution. Therefore no threshold-dependent binary failure-frontier claim is promoted after the fact.

Permitted use:
- condition-level target-runtime and scaling characterization;
- discovery versus held-out consistency described without retrofitted threshold selection;
- bounded statements about evaluated BBOB conditions and frozen configurations.

Not permitted:
- a post hoc exact frontier threshold;
- a universal failure boundary;
- a G4/G5 repair claim inferred solely from deterioration;
- selecting a remedy after seeing confirmation outcomes.

This conservative interpretation preserves the successful held-out experiment while preventing post-result specification from being presented as confirmatory evidence.
