# Analysis Endpoints

## Confirmatory
- target attainment at frozen target/budget combinations;
- evaluations-to-target;
- paired algorithm contrasts on matched conditions;
- failure-frontier crossing under a frozen reliability criterion.

## Descriptive/supporting
- median/best-so-far distributions;
- ECDF/anytime curves;
- runtime and optimizer overhead;
- population/diversity diagnostics where available;
- rank summaries.

## Exploratory
- mechanism-state predictors;
- post-hoc clustering;
- unexpected interaction patterns;
- candidate new failure regimes.

Exploratory findings may generate a new predeclared validation experiment; they are not silently relabeled confirmatory.
