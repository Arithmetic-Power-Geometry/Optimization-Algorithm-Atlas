# Nelder–Mead Evidence Card

## Mechanism
Derivative-free simplex transformations use only objective values to reflect, expand, contract or shrink a simplex.

## Established boundary
Classical theory does not provide a general high-dimensional convergence guarantee, and counterexamples show convergence to nonminimizing points can occur even for smooth convex problems in two dimensions.

## Current signal
Recent 2026 work continues to study poor high-dimensional behavior through simplex-state dynamics, including persistent reflection dominance and distorted simplex geometry.

## Atlas question
Can simplex geometry/state variables predict practical failure before stagnation, and which modern control/regularization remedies genuinely extend the reliable dimensional regime?

Status: documented theoretical limitation; empirical failure frontier remains to be mapped.
