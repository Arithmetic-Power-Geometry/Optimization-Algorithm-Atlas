# Simulated Annealing Evidence Card

## Mechanism
A temperature-controlled stochastic acceptance rule permits uphill moves, with exploration progressively reduced by the cooling schedule.

## Established tradeoff
Classical simulated annealing has strong asymptotic motivation under appropriate schedules, but slow convergence is a longstanding practical limitation. Many accelerated/adaptive schedules trade theoretical conservatism for finite-budget efficiency.

## Atlas question
Map the finite-budget frontier among cooling aggressiveness, landscape barrier structure, dimension and success probability rather than treating convergence-in-the-limit as practical efficiency.

Status: mature family; gap focus is finite-budget mechanism behavior, not invention of another schedule without evidence.
