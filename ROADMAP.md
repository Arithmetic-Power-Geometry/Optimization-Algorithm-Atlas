# Research Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Deep evidence mining
Freeze review questions and inclusion rules; expand the canonical registry; run one uniform audit per algorithm/family; populate source and claim ledgers; reconstruct mechanism ancestry; record negative evidence; audit benchmark coverage; validate candidate gaps.

## Phase 2 — Mechanism and benchmark atlas
Normalize mechanism features, calculate structural similarity, create genealogy/timeline, annotate benchmark properties, and generate the algorithm × condition evidence matrix.

## Phase 3 — Controlled comparisons
Select comparisons from validated gaps rather than desired winners; lock budgets and seeds; calibrate implementations; run standardized suites; retain raw run-level results.

## Phase 4 — Failure frontiers
Systematically vary problem properties and dimensions; estimate and replicate regime transitions; distinguish tuning failure from mechanism failure.

## Phase 5 — Gap repair
Test established remedies first. If unresolved, derive a mechanism from the validated failure, perform ablation, and test outside the discovery regime.

## Phase 6 — Artifact freeze
Archive raw results; regenerate all figures/tables; validate references and claim-evidence links; freeze versions and checksums.

## Phase 7 — Paper
Write the paper only from frozen evidence and artifacts.
