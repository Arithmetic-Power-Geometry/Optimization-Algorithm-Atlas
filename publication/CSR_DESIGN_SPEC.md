# Computer Science Review — Design Specification

## Article identity
Critical systematic review + reproducible evidence atlas + computational gap validation.

## Central thesis
Optimization research should be evaluated at the level of **mechanism × condition × evidence**, not algorithm name × aggregate benchmark rank.

## Required sections
1. Why another optimization review is necessary
2. Review protocol and evidence ontology
3. Historical mechanism evolution
4. Cross-paradigm mechanism taxonomy
5. Algorithm DNA and structural similarity
6. Benchmark-property coverage
7. Condition-level evidence atlas
8. Reproducibility and implementation evidence
9. Counterevidence and documented failure regimes
10. Controlled failure-frontier experiments
11. Research-gap atlas and resolving experiments
12. Researcher decision framework
13. Limitations and threats to validity
14. Research agenda
15. Conclusions

## Main-text design
The main paper should synthesize; exhaustive records remain in supplementary machine-readable artifacts.

## Evidence discipline
Every strong claim must map to:
- primary/canonical source where relevant;
- condition metadata for empirical claims;
- contradictory evidence where found;
- project artifact for project-generated findings.

## Review methodology
Maintain reproducible search strings, dates, databases, screening decisions, deduplication, inclusion/exclusion reasons, source roles, extraction fields and evidence confidence.

## Original computational component
Use experiments to resolve review-discovered uncertainties rather than to manufacture a leaderboard.

## Novel algorithm
Not required. If a validated G4/G5 gap survives established remedies, a new transferable mechanism may be evaluated as a separate case study or subsequent paper.
