# Write-Paper Gate

## Current decision
**DO NOT WRITE THE PAPER YET.**

The paper is downstream of the evidence system. Drafting early risks locking the project into claims before the evidence map and failure-frontier experiments are complete.

## Mandatory gate
Paper drafting may start only when:
- closest-prior-review audit is current;
- declared census scope is complete;
- canonical references are verified;
- mechanism fingerprints and similarity analysis are complete;
- benchmark-property audit is complete;
- evidence cells and counterevidence are populated;
- reproducibility audit is complete;
- statistical protocol is frozen;
- planned experiments and stress tests are complete;
- candidate gaps are promoted/rejected;
- established remedies are tested for any G4/G5 gap;
- final figures/tables regenerate from frozen data;
- final references are verified;
- clean-workflow rebuild passes;
- evidence freeze is recorded.

## Allowed before gate
Methods notes, schemas, artifact captions, table/figure specifications, evidence summaries and repository documentation.

## Not allowed before gate
Final abstract, final conclusions, final novelty claims, performance claims based on incomplete experiments, or a manuscript narrative that pressures the evidence to fit it.

## Quality target
Journal quartile and acceptance cannot be guaranteed. The controllable target is a methodologically rigorous, auditable, broadly useful review suitable for evaluation at a strong Q1 review venue.
