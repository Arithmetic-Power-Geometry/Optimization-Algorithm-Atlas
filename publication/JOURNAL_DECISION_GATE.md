# Journal Decision Gate

The target venue is finalized only after evidence freeze.

## Route A — Computer Science Review (current primary)
Use when the final contribution remains cross-paradigm:
- mathematical/local optimization;
- gradient/quasi-Newton/proximal methods;
- derivative-free methods;
- evolutionary and swarm methods;
- Bayesian/surrogate optimization;
- multi/many-objective methods;
- discrete/dynamic/robust/learning-assisted optimization.

Required framing: critical systematic review + mechanism/evidence atlas + reproducible computational validation of review-discovered uncertainty.

## Route B — IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation
Use when the final scientific center of gravity becomes evolutionary/population-based optimization, especially if failure-frontier experiments and mechanism analysis primarily concern DE, ES/CMA-ES, GA, PSO, EDA and related methods.

Required framing: evolutionary-computation mechanisms, theory/behavior, benchmark evidence, methodological implications and broadly useful conclusions.

## Decision criteria at P031
1. proportion of final evidence cells by paradigm;
2. scope of the strongest validated result;
3. whether the principal contribution is a general CS review or an EC-specific scientific synthesis;
4. closest published surveys in the candidate venue;
5. manuscript length/format compatibility;
6. editor/readership fit.

No journal is selected because of brand alone. Scope fit and the final evidence determine submission.
