# Paper Start Checklist

The manuscript starts only after P031 Evidence Freeze.

## At the moment drafting starts, the following must already exist
- frozen review questions and eligibility criteria;
- final literature-search cutoff date;
- closest-review differentiation audit;
- complete declared census;
- verified canonical and claim-supporting bibliography;
- stable mechanism taxonomy and sensitivity-tested similarity analysis;
- benchmark-property audit;
- condition-level evidence matrix including counterevidence;
- theory/guarantee matrix;
- reproducibility audit;
- completed predeclared computational experiments;
- validated/rejected gap registry;
- existing-remedy audit for any G4/G5 gaps;
- final generated figures and tables;
- artifact checksums and clean-workflow regeneration;
- evidence-freeze release.

## Writing order after freeze
Do not write from Section 1 to Section 16 sequentially.

Recommended order:
1. Methods / review protocol.
2. Mechanism taxonomy and definitions.
3. Evidence-atlas sections.
4. Theory/reproducibility sections.
5. Failure-frontier and gap sections.
6. Researcher decision framework and research frontier.
7. Limitations.
8. Introduction.
9. Conclusion.
10. Abstract.
11. Title.
12. Highlights / graphical abstract / cover letter if required.

This order minimizes narrative claims made before the evidence sections are stable.

## Drafting rule
Every paragraph containing a substantive claim must be traceable to a source record, frozen review synthesis, or generated artifact.
