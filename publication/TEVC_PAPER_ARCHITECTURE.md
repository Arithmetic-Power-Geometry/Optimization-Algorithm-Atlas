# TEVC-Oriented Paper Architecture

Activated only after P031 Evidence Freeze and only if final scope is compatible with IEEE Transactions on Evolutionary Computation.

1. Introduction
2. Review Questions, Scope, and Evidence Methodology
3. Evolutionary and Population-Based Mechanism Taxonomy
4. Mechanism Genealogy and Structural Similarity
5. Benchmark Ecosystems and Experimental Practice
6. Condition-Level Evidence Atlas
7. Theory, Guarantees, and Known Failure Modes
8. Reproducibility and Experimental Credibility
9. Controlled Failure-Frontier Experiments
10. Mechanism–Failure Relationships
11. Validated Gaps, Existing Remedies, and Unresolved Questions
12. Research and Evaluation Recommendations
13. Threats to Validity
14. Conclusion

## Scope rule
If P031 shows that the strongest contribution remains genuinely cross-paradigm beyond evolutionary/population-based optimization, retain the broader architecture and reconsider the target venue rather than forcing the evidence into TEVC scope.

## Writing order after freeze
Methods → taxonomy/genealogy → evidence atlas → theory/reproducibility → experiments → gaps/remedies → recommendations → limitations → introduction → conclusion → abstract → title.
