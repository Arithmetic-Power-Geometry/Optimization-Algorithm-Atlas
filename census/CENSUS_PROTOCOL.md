# Optimization Census Protocol

## Purpose
Build broad historical coverage without treating every published algorithm name as an independent computational idea.

## Census unit
Each record is first an algorithm **identity**, then linked to a normalized mechanism fingerprint.

## Inclusion lanes
A. Foundational mathematical/local optimization
B. Gradient, stochastic-gradient and second-order optimization
C. Derivative-free/direct/model-based optimization
D. Evolutionary computation
E. Differential-evolution lineage
F. Swarm/collective optimization
G. Other metaphor-labelled metaheuristics
H. Probabilistic/EDA/cross-entropy methods
I. Surrogate/Bayesian expensive optimization
J. Multiobjective/many-objective optimization
K. Constrained optimization
L. Robust/stochastic optimization
M. Dynamic/online optimization
N. Mixed/discrete/combinatorial optimization
O. Bilevel/multilevel optimization
P. Large-scale optimization
Q. Learning-to-optimize / meta-black-box optimization

## Role assignment
Each identity receives one role:
- FOUNDATION — historically or theoretically foundational;
- CANONICAL — canonical representative of a distinct mechanism;
- MAJOR_VARIANT — materially changes a mechanism or capability;
- SPECIALIST — important for a defined problem regime;
- REDUNDANCY_AUDIT — included primarily to assess structural overlap;
- EMERGING — recent mechanism with insufficient mature evidence;
- CATALOG_ONLY — recorded for completeness but not given a full narrative card.

## Priority rule
Narrative depth follows scientific distinctiveness and evidence value, not popularity or metaphor novelty.

## Required provenance
No census identity becomes VERIFIED until title/authors/year/venue/DOI or equivalent canonical bibliographic record is checked.

## Variant rule
A parameter tweak, renaming, hybridization or metaphor change is not automatically a new mechanism.

## Paper use
The full census belongs in machine-readable supplement. The paper synthesizes clusters, canonical representatives, important variants and evidence boundaries.
